ABOUT SYNK CORP
Synk Corp is a software and AI development company based in Baneshwor, Kathmandu, Nepal. The company says it builds websites, mobile apps, AI systems, and automation for businesses in Nepal and internationally.

TAGLINE
“Think it. Synk IT.”

SERVICES
1. Web development
   - Company websites and web applications.
   - The website mentions Next.js, CMS, and SEO as relevant technologies/capabilities.

2. Mobile app development
   - iOS and Android apps.
   - The website mentions Flutter, Swift, and Kotlin.

3. UI/UX design
   - User-centered interface design, wireframes, prototypes, and design systems.
   - Figma is listed among the technologies/tools.

4. AI development and automation
   - Custom AI applications and AI-powered internal tools.
   - AI agents and workflows for multi-step tasks such as working with emails, sheets, and CRMs.
   - AI integration into existing software.
   - Document and data AI for extracting, summarizing, and searching documents such as PDFs, invoices, forms, and reports.
   - Knowledge assistants using retrieval-augmented generation (RAG).
   - AI consulting and training.
   - The AI services page lists Claude, OpenAI, Python, LangChain, TensorFlow, vector databases, and n8n among its technologies.

5. AI chatbots and Synk Bot
   - Synk Corp presents Synk Bot as an AI-powered automation product for social-media engagement and lead generation.
   - The website describes Instagram and Facebook direct-message automation, AI-powered replies, instant responses, and lead generation.
   - The homepage describes 24/7 automated engagement. Treat performance figures and marketing claims as website claims, not independently verified guarantees.

6. E-commerce and billing systems
   - Custom online stores and e-commerce websites/apps.
   - Billing and invoicing software, including VAT/PAN invoice formats.
   - Point-of-sale (POS) systems for retail and restaurants.
   - Inventory and stock management.
   - The website lists payment options such as eSewa, Khalti, Fonepay QR, bank transfer, cash on delivery, and international cards.
   - Sales dashboards and reports.

7. Other listed service areas
   - Fintech and wallet apps.
   - Healthcare apps.
   - Education apps.

HOW SYNK CORP SAYS IT WORKS
The company describes the following process:
1. Discovery: understand business goals, audience, and technical requirements.
2. Design: prepare wireframes, prototypes, and visual designs.
3. Development: build the product using appropriate technologies and practices.
4. Testing: perform quality assurance across devices and edge cases.
5. Launch: deploy the product and monitor performance.
6. Support: provide maintenance, updates, fixes, and improvements.

CONSULTATION
The website offers a free initial consultation. It says the consultation can help discuss the project, suggest an approach, and provide a scope, timeline, and quote. The website says there is no obligation to continue.
